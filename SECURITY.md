# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting:
open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Don't open a
public issue, and please give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before sharing it.

Helpful reports include the affected route or page, steps to reproduce, and the impact you
see. We'll acknowledge reports within a few days and keep you updated until a fix is out.

## Scope

In scope: the ATSly application in this repository, including authentication, data
ownership (one person reaching another's resumes, scans or account), file upload and
parsing, rate limits, and the handling of resume text sent to AI providers.

Out of scope: denial of service through volume alone, reports that need a compromised
device or browser, and issues in third-party services themselves.
